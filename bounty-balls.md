# Bounty Balls   

## Ingredients
- 2 cups desiccated coconut
- 1 can sweetened condensed milk (395g)
- 1/2 cup chocolate drops (white, milk or dark)
  
## Method
1. Combine coconut and condensed milk in a bowl
1. Chill for 1 hour
1. Roll mixture into 2cm balls and place on lined tray
1. Freeze for 1+ hours
1. Melt chocolate (bain-marie or microwave 2 x 30 sec bursts stirring well in between)
1. Cover balls with chocolate and chill/freeze
